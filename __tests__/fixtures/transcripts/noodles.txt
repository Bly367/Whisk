Hey guys, today we're making 15 minute garlic chili noodles. You need about 200 grams of noodles, 4 cloves of garlic minced, 2 green onions, a tablespoon of chili flakes, 2 tablespoons of soy sauce, 1 tablespoon of black vinegar and a teaspoon of sugar. Boil your noodles according to the package. While those cook, put the garlic, green onions and chili flakes in a bowl. Heat 3 tablespoons of oil until it's smoking and pour it right over the top. It's gonna sizzle. Mix in the soy sauce, vinegar and sugar, then toss with the drained noodles. So good.
