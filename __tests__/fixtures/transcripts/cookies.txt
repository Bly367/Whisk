These are the best brown butter chocolate chip cookies you'll ever make. Start by browning 1 cup of butter, that's two sticks, until it smells nutty, and let it cool. Then whisk in 1 cup of brown sugar, half a cup of white sugar, 2 eggs and 2 teaspoons of vanilla. Now add 2 and 1/4 cups of flour, 1 teaspoon of baking soda and a teaspoon of salt, and fold in 2 cups of chocolate chips. Chill the dough for at least 30 minutes. Scoop them onto a sheet and bake at 350 for 10 to 12 minutes. Let them cool on the tray. Comment cookie if you want the full recipe.
