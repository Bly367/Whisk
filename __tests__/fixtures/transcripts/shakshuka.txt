Alright this is the shakshuka I make every single Sunday. We're gonna start with a big glug of olive oil in a skillet, and in goes one diced onion and a red bell pepper. Once those are soft, I'm adding three cloves of garlic, a teaspoon of cumin and a teaspoon of smoked paprika. Pour in a 28 ounce can of crushed tomatoes and let it bubble away for about ten minutes. Make little wells with your spoon and crack in four eggs. Pop the lid on and cook until the whites are set, maybe six to eight minutes. Finish it with crumbled feta and some fresh parsley, and serve it with crusty bread. Honestly so good.
