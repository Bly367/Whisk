Air fryer teriyaki salmon, dinner in fifteen minutes. For the sauce, mix together a quarter cup of soy sauce, two tablespoons of honey, one tablespoon of rice vinegar, a teaspoon of grated ginger and one minced garlic clove. Pat two salmon fillets dry and brush half of the sauce over the top. Air fry them at 400 degrees for eight to ten minutes. While that's cooking, simmer the rest of the sauce in a small pan until it thickens. Drizzle it over the salmon and sprinkle with sesame seeds and sliced green onions. Serve it over rice.
