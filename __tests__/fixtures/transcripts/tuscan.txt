Okay so this is my creamy Tuscan chicken and it's ridiculously easy. So first you're gonna season two chicken breasts with salt, pepper and a teaspoon of garlic powder. Get a pan nice and hot with a tablespoon of olive oil and sear them for about five minutes each side, then take them out. In the same pan I'm adding three cloves of garlic, a handful of sun-dried tomatoes and then one cup of heavy cream and half a cup of parmesan. Let that simmer for a couple minutes, throw in a big handful of spinach until it wilts, and then put the chicken back in. Spoon that sauce over the top and that's dinner.
