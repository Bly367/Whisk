Creamy, Spicy Tortellini and Sausage Soup ❤️‍🔥

This one’s comfort in a bowl. Think creamy tomato soup meets cheesy tortellini meets the savory goodness of Italian sausage — all simmered together until it’s rich, silky, and perfectly cozy.

Ingredients below, full recipe in my bio!

1 tbsp unsalted butter
1 tbsp olive oil
1 lb Italian sausage (hot or mild)
1 medium yellow onion, finely diced
2 stalks celery, finely diced (about ½ cup)
2 medium carrots, finely diced (about ½ cup)
6–8 cloves garlic, minced, to taste
1 tsp dried basil
1 tsp dried oregano
½ tsp dried thyme
½ tsp dried rosemary
1–2 tsp Calabrian chilies or red pepper flakes, to taste
3 tbsp tomato paste
3 tbsp all-purpose flour
½ cup dry white wine
28 oz crushed tomatoes
4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
1 Parmesan rind (optional)
1 bay leaf
½ cup heavy cream, room temperature
2 cups spinach (or kale for heartier texture)
10–12 oz cheese tortellini
1 cup freshly grated Parmesan, plus more to finish
Lemon zest or a squeeze of lemon juice (optional, for brightness)
Fresh basil, for garnish
