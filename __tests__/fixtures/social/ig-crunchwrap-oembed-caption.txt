Crunchwrap Supreme 🌯🔥

Homemade, extra spicy, and crunchier than the drive-thru 🌶️

Beef

450g ground beef
½ onion, diced
1 pack Taco Bell seasoning @tacobell
1 tsp cayenne

Assembly

4 large tortillas
4 small tortillas (to close the bottom)
4 tostadas
Shredded cheddar + mozzarella
Sour cream
Lettuce, tomato, jalapeño
Oil for the pan

Instructions

Cook the beef with the onion until browned, then add the Taco Bell seasoning and cayenne. Cook until everything is well combined and the beef is fully seasoned.

Chop the lettuce, tomato, and jalapeño. Lay out a large tortilla and add the beef in the center, followed by the cheddar and mozzarella. Top with a tostada, spread on sour cream, and add the veggies. Place a small tortilla over the filling to cover the bottom, then fold the edges of the large tortilla into the center.

Place it seam side down in an oiled pan over medium heat. Crisp up for 2–3 minutes per side until golden. Enjoy 😮‍💨
