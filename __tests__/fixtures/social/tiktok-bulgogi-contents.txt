GROUND BEEF BULGOGI
A quick, savory Korean‑inspired beef bowl that’s perfect for weeknights.

INGREDIENTS
Main
1 lb ground beef
1 onion, sliced
1 tbsp minced garlic
Oil, for cooking
Sesame oil, for finishing
Sesame seeds, garnish
Chopped scallion, garnish
Kimchi or radish kimchi, for serving

Sauce
2 tbsp brown sugar
2 tbsp mirin
4 tbsp soy sauce (low sodium preferred)
1 tbsp Korean plum syrup*
*Sub: 1 tbsp water + 1 tsp apple cider vinegar

INSTRUCTIONS
1. Peel and slice the onion; set aside.

2. Combine brown sugar, mirin, soy sauce, and plum syrup (or substitute). Mix well and set aside.

3. Heat a little oil in a pan. Add sliced onions and cook for 1 minute. Add minced garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant.

4. Add ground beef and break it into small pieces. Once the beef is about 30% cooked, pour in the sauce. Let it simmer until the sauce reduces and evaporates — stir occasionally. (This part takes a little time, but it’s worth it.)

5. Drizzle with sesame oil and give it a final mix.

6. Assemble your bowl with rice, scallions, sesame seeds, and kimchi/ veggie of choice. Enjoy!
