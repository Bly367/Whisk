# Privacy Policy (Whisk)

_Last updated: July 31, 2026_

Whisk helps you save, organize, and cook recipes. This policy explains what data we process.

## Data we store

- Account email and authentication credentials (handled by Supabase Auth)
- Recipe library content you save (ingredients, steps, images, folders, meal plans, grocery lists)
- Import telemetry without recipe content: import kind, source category, status, duration, and error codes

## How we use data

- Sync your recipes across devices when you sign in
- Structure recipe text/photos with AI when you import from links or images
- Enforce daily import quotas and prevent abuse

## AI and photo processing

When you import a URL or cookbook photo, relevant text or image data is sent to our secure backend and an AI provider to extract recipe structure. We do not intentionally store raw captions, page HTML, or photo bytes in telemetry tables.

## Storage and security

Recipe images are stored in a private bucket. Access is limited to your account via authentication and path-scoped policies. Sessions are stored securely on device.

## Your choices

- Export your library as JSON from Account
- Delete your account and associated cloud recipe data from Account
- Sign out to stop cloud sync on that device
